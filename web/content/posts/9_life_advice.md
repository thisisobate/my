I turn 27 today. It's been 9 years since I became an adult. I have made a good number of decisions so far. While some turned out to be lessons, I've been fortunate enough to make some really good decisions that have made my life successful.

Here are 9 good decisions you need to make to build a young, rich life:

1. Build your faith in God
2. Surround yourself with ambitious people
3. Embrace your identity
4. Learn to read
5. Learn to write
6. Fall in love with a sport
7. Pick one viable skill and commit 10,000 hours to honing it
8. Go to the gym at least twice a week
9. Learn personal finance

I'm hoping to go into more detail on each of these points in my podcast, so stay tuned!
